About

John Lewis was a civil rights activist who famously said,
"Speak up, speak out, get in the way. Get in good trouble, necessary trouble, and help redeem the soul of America."

The Good Trouble Alliance strives to live up to this ideal as we peacefully fight authoritarianism and human rights abuses in America. We aim to be the spark that ignites a revolution of freedom and human decency.

We don't just participate in democracy. We fight back. We're here to topple the regime. We strategize bold, creative, nonviolent initiatives we can implement locally. We don’t ask for change — we make it.
